## Summary of Consumer Complaints & Dealership Practices

Purchasing a vehicle from **Kia of Cape Coral** turned into a complete logistical, mechanical, and customer service nightmare. Prospective buyers should be aware of how this dealership handles sales agreements, vehicle preparation, post-sale damage, and customer accountability.

### 1. Breach of Written Sales Contracts & Wrap Removal Failures
* **The Issue:** Purchased a vehicle that was wrapped, making the underlying paint condition invisible. A binding, **written condition of the sale** required the dealership to professionally remove the wrap prior to delivery.
* **The Dealership Response:** After the transaction was finalized, management repeatedly attempted to back out of their contractual obligation, trying to hand over inadequate payout checks to avoid doing the labor themselves. 
* **Lack of Accountability:** General Manager **Ryan Gambill** and dealership staff failed to return phone calls or take responsibility for honoring signed contracts.

### 2. Severe Vehicle Damage & Improper Paint Prep
* **Clear Coat Destruction:** When forced to honor the contract, the dealership stripped the wrap with zero proper preparation, destroying the vehicle's clear coat, leaving heavy glue residue over the entire body, and leaving scraps of wrap sticking out from panels.
* **Concealing Damage:** Management attempted to hide the extent of the damage until the buyer arrived in person to take delivery, only agreeing to address it after the buyer **refused to drive the car off the lot**.
* **Misaligned Reassembly & Overspray:** After attempting a botched "fix," the vehicle was returned with a completely misaligned tailgate, broken sensors (triggering constant open-trunk warnings), and overspray from careless paint correction.

### 3. Out-of-Pocket Financial Burden & Mechanical Risks
* **Third-Party Repair Costs:** The buyer was forced to take the car directly to a certified **Toyota dealership**, paying **$900 out of pocket** just to fix dangerous reassembly errors, loose/missing bolts, and diagnostic issues.
* **Carfax & Structural Concerns:** The sloppy condition and mechanical overspray were severe enough that the servicing dealer had to pull a **Carfax report** to check if the vehicle had been secretly wrecked.
* **Detailing Costs:** Additional funds had to be spent on professional exterior detailing to completely remove the stubborn glue residue left behind by Kia of Cape Coral's detail crew.
